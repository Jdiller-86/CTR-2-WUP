# Native and GX diagnostics

Build @VERSION@, source @SOURCE_COMMIT@. FTP the contents of sd-card/ to the SD root in binary mode, close FTP and launch the requested app in Aroma.

For Native, complete only the requested on-screen checks: R confirms, L records a problem, ZL skips and Minus exits. For GX Transfers, expect green/red/green on TV and blue on GamePad; confirm with R or record a problem with L, then Minus to exit.

FTP the whole sd:/ctr2wup/results/ folder to the computer, ZIP it and return it with a brief observation. Include partial failures. Source, reference frames, notices and receipts are under support/; keep games, saves and keys out of the return.
