# Audio Check and GX Transfers

Build @VERSION@, source @SOURCE_COMMIT@. FTP the contents of sd-card/ to the SD root in binary mode, close FTP and launch the requested app in Aroma.

For Audio Check, confirm launch silence, press A for the short TV/GamePad tone, then R for correct sound or L for a problem. For GX Transfers, expect green/red/green on TV and blue on GamePad; confirm with R or record a problem with L. Press Minus after the requested check.

FTP the whole sd:/ctr2wup/results/ folder to the computer, ZIP it and return it with a brief picture/sound/exit observation. Include partial failures and any displayed error. Keep games, saves and keys out of the return.
